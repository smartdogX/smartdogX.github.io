## 2024-08-16 07:51:25

For a long time, I have wanted to write something—something private, unrelated to any of my identities, something that belongs only to me. But I never found a suitable place to record it. Today it suddenly occurred to me that I could build a personal website, so I searched for information, and that is how this website—or blog, I suppose—came about. At first, I thought the idea was quite original. Only after searching did I realize that people had been doing it for ages. Coming up with something entirely new really is too difficult. I chose WordPress more or less at random, because the company seemed to have the largest market share. For now, I do not plan to pay for a membership, so there is a banner at the top of the page. I do not like it much; maybe I will get a membership later. I have rambled on quite a bit, but I have finally done something I have wanted to do for a long time, and that makes me pretty happy. From now on, I will try to keep writing. Hello world!

## 2024-08-16 12:10:17

I am so excited. Being able to write something anytime, anywhere feels wonderful. I used to have all sorts of thoughts and then simply forget them. It is not that I never thought about recording them somehow; I just never actually did it. Thinking about it carefully, it is really the blog format that attracted me and made me decide to write my thoughts down. A blog is different from public writing on WeChat Moments or Weibo, and different from writing in a notes app or a personal notebook. First, it feels pretty cool, because not many people use one. Second, a blog is very private while still leaving open the possibility of being seen by someone else. That is what is truly exciting. It is like a message in a bottle by the sea: the point is not really what you write, but that it might cross an ocean and be read by another person. A blog is the same. Maybe one day I will share this blog with people, or maybe I have subconsciously had that idea from the very beginning. Who knows?

Next, I need to stick with it and try to write a little every day. Since I have plenty of time lately, I could learn some HTML or something and make the website better. Blogs can have pictures too, but for now I think I will avoid using it to document my daily life. I think those things belong on WeChat Moments.

One thing to bear in mind is that a blog is not an entirely naked, truthful reflection of life, at least for me. Jiang Wen once said, "What decent person keeps a diary?" There is a deep question here about how people perceive their own morality. I am hardly going to expose my dark side or all my thoughts without holding anything back. After all, even with ourselves, there is a tendency toward moral self-deception, because everyone wants their image of themselves to be good—which is obviously impossible, even when facing only themselves.

Finally, a few thoughts about life. I need to work hard lately and try to learn more, which should help with my applications too. My plan for the next few days is to finish learning field theory and write something about it on Zhihu. Keep going.
